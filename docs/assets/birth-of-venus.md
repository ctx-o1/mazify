# Sample painting

The sample is Sandro Botticelli’s *The Birth of Venus*, circa 1485, in the Uffizi.
[Uffizi artwork record](https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/birth-of-venus)

The 1280 × 804 JPEG at `public/examples/birth-of-venus.jpg` is a public-domain
reproduction from Wikimedia Commons, sourced from Google Art Project. It is
served locally without cropping or colour changes.
[Image record and licensing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sandro_Botticelli_-_La_nascita_di_Venere_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg)
